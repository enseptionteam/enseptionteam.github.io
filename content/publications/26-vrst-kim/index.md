abstract: 'Obstacle negotiation means stepping over, crossing, or passing through
  an obstacle while regulating gait and foot placement. It differs from detecting
  one or steering around it, and is hard to study once vision is unavailable. We
  present a walkable VR testbed for this task. Participants traverse a bounded lane
  between two tactile mats and negotiate procedurally sized steps, trenches, and
  gaps with the display fully occluded, while head, waist, and foot tracking drive
  a full-body digital twin. Sensory substitution conveys the obstacle as sound through
  the foundational vOICe mapping. Sessions with thirteen sighted participants indicate
  that audibility and negotiation difficulty resist separation, stalling the adaptive
  staircase on two of three obstacle classes.'
